Whisper Integration for Natural Language Understanding

Introduction​

Natural Language Understanding (NLU) is crucial for intuitive Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). This chapter focuses on integrating advanced speech-to-text models, specifically OpenAI's Whisper, into robotic systems to enable robots to understand spoken commands and engage in more natural dialogue. We will explore how Whisper can convert human speech into text, which can then be processed for cognitive planning and action execution.

Theory​

OpenAI Whisper is a general-purpose speech recognition model. It is trained on a large dataset of diverse audio and is also a multi-task model that can perform multilingual speech recognition, speech translation, and language identification. Its key advantages include:

  • High Accuracy: Performs exceptionally well across various accents, background noise, and languages.
  • Multilingual Support: Can transcribe and translate speech in many languages.
  • Robustness: Handles imperfect audio conditions effectively.

Integrating Whisper into a robotics stack involves:

  1. Audio Capture: Recording human speech using a microphone connected to the robot or a connected system.
  2. Speech-to-Text Conversion: Feeding the audio to Whisper (either a local model or an API endpoint) to get a text transcript.
  3. Natural Language Processing (NLP): Analyzing the text transcript to extract commands, entities, and intent.
  4. Action Mapping: Translating the NLU output into specific robotic actions or modifications to the cognitive plan.
